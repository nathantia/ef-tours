The itinerary, made useful

What students will do, learn, and apply.

Each city connects a real experience to a learning goal and a simple classroom application.

Days 2–3

London

Walk Westminster and the Thames, visit major civic sites, and learn to navigate the city as a group.

What you’ll learnHow monuments, public space, and ceremony communicate national identity and power.
Use it in classCompare Westminster with a public space in your own city, using photos as evidence.
Days 4–6

Paris

Visit the Louvre, explore neighborhood streets, and observe how art, food, and public life shape the city.

What you’ll learnHow art and everyday routines reveal cultural values, history, and identity.
Use it in classExplain how seeing one artwork or object in context changed your interpretation of it.
Days 7–9

Rome

Stand inside the Colosseum and Roman Forum and gather evidence from the buildings, roads, and public spaces around you.

What you’ll learnHow engineering, public entertainment, and civic space shaped Roman society.
Use it in classBuild an argument about Roman power using your own observations and images from the site.
