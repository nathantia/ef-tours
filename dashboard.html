Your learning summary

How Japan changed the way I think about technology and connection.

The moment

On the shinkansen from the Fuji-Hakone region toward Kyoto, a complex transportation system felt remarkably simple from the traveler’s point of view.

What I learned

Technology can shape daily life most powerfully when infrastructure, design, and human behavior work together so smoothly that the complexity almost disappears.

How I’ll apply it

In economics or geography class, I can compare Japan’s high-speed rail with transportation at home and look at how infrastructure affects mobility, regional economies, and access to opportunity.
